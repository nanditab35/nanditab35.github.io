Independent Explorations in AI

This space documents my personal journey through the evolving landscape of Artificial Intelligence. Here, I dive deep into the technical intricacies and practical applications that fascinate me, driven purely by curiosity and a passion for understanding how these systems truly work.

My writing focuses on independent research and solo project work—these explorations are my own intellectual pursuits, separate from my professional roles. They represent nights, weekends, and moments of inspiration spent building, testing, and deconstructing concepts to satisfy my own need for learning.

Current Areas of Deep Dive:

  • The architectural nuances of Transformers & LLMs—peering inside the "black box"
  • Practical implementations of Fine-tuning, RAG, and Multi-modal systems for real-world scenarios
  • The creative and perceptual frontiers of Computer Vision, Diffusion Models, and Multi-modal Learning

  • The essential scaffolding of MLOps/LLMOps & Containerization that turns prototypes into reliable systems
  • The imperative of Responsible & Explainable AI, ensuring innovation is matched with integrity

This blog is my thinking ground—where theory meets my own hands-on experimentation. It's a chronicle of questions asked, code written, and insights gained on a solo journey through the depths of modern AI.
