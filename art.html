My Art Journal

  • Childhood Inspirations: These were the childhood inspirations for painting that deeply touched me at a conscious and subconscious-level: Childhood hobby, Journey with Sketching, Pastel Colors, Water Color. Love for kid's story books with pictures, especially the Epics like Ramayan, Mahabharat, and kid's books on Ramkrishna Dev, Sharada Ma, Swami Vivekananda with beautiful pictures.

  • Painting Teacher: This Art blog is dedicated to my Painting teacher - "Babul Das", A Great Painting Guru. Always passionate about teaching others about painting. He was an expert of Fine Arts, having a degree from Govt. Art College, Kolkata.

  • Lack of Oil painting experience - earlier years: well I was always fascinated by it, but back then I could not find enough time to learn it from our teacher. I learnt mainly Pastel and Watercolor and a little bit of Acrylic medium from our Art Teacher. Left Art classes at 9th Standard (2001) of School. But our teacher said that "If you ever do painting at any stage of your life, then that will be my reward/Guru Dakshina".

  • Artist's block: After leaving the Art classes, I have faced with a long break from painting. With time, I completely lost the old passion for painting. So much so, that I was convinced that I don't have it in me anymore. I was scared of the blank page, and the fear was mainly about the fact that I would probably waste the page and colours because of my inabilities.

  • Revival of Art Inspiration: Years passed, then came the COVID-19 pandemic. Lock-down & Work-from-Home became the new normal. In 2021, suddenly, I re-discovered my passion for painting by accidentally and casually watching Lena Danya’s (A renowned oil-painter) Youtube videos on painting process and time-lapse [Lena Danya (YT Channel)] regarding oil painting, especially her Oiltober-series of mini-paintings [4x6 inch]. I was so fascinated by how she is sharing her process of painting, details regarding art supplies and curating of references etc, that I decided to try again.

  • Baby-steps to come out of Artist’s block: I started with mini-paintings, since this idea helped me to reduce my fear of wasting the page as the space in the page was less. This sounds funny, but sometimes the mind works in funny ways.

  • I surprised myself: After 20 years, my painting journey re-started, as if it was always there. And it started with Acrylic and Oil painting this time. I had no prior experience particularly with oil painting, but with all previous knowledge with other mediums, repeated watching of Lena’s Painting Videos and of course with God’s grace and our painting teacher’s good wishes, it clicked. Then it started flowing naturally.

  • Inspiration for loose-painting style: I also watched a lot of YouTube videos from Chelsea Lang (A renowned portrait painter, instructor) Youtube videos on painting time-lapse and many insights [Chelsea Lang (YT Channel)]. This inspired me to try and explore more portraits and loose painting styles.

  • Exploring my own style, while connecting to my true-self: I am still exploring my own style for oil-painting. Painting is another form of meditation for me. It helps me to unwind and works as a stress buster. Currently my painting style is something in-between tight and loose painting. It just flows naturally, whichever comes, I just enjoy it, in that moment. It feels like a moment of connection to your own self, or God. Like Lena said in one of her videos, “Enjoy the Process of Painting, Don't worry about the Product”.
